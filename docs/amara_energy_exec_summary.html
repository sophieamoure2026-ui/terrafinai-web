01 / The Opportunity

Amara Energy AB is a fast-growing, pan-Nordic geothermal energy platform. Operating at the intersection of energy transition and real assets, Amara is executing a highly disciplined, acquisition-led industrial consolidation strategy. The company acquires profitable, family-owned geo-energy operators across Sweden and Norway, integrating them onto a single pan-Nordic drilling, engineering, and data platform to capture significant scaling efficiencies and prepare for a public listing in Stockholm.

02 / Investment Highlights
  • Proven Consolidation Strategy: Three profitable acquisitions completed to date, with a proprietary pipeline of approximately 100 target operators identified across the Nordics.
  • Real Operational Assets: SEK 400 million in tangible assets already on the balance sheet, ensuring the investment is backed by cash-generating operations rather than speculative technology.
  • Oslo Airport Geothermal System: Owns and operates premium projects, including the landmark Oslo Airport geothermal heating and cooling system, which has been in continuous, stable operation since 2018.
  • Full Vertical Integration: Possesses comprehensive internal drilling, geo-engineering, digital twin, and real-time operational monitoring capabilities.
03 / Transaction Structure & Rights
  • Offering Details: SEK 30,000,000 in new equity at a pre-money valuation of SEK 600,000,000.
  • Investor Protections: Standard straight equity shares featuring full voting and dividend rights equal to existing shareholders.
  • Follow-on Rights: Subscribing investors receive pro-rata allocation rights in subsequent funding rounds.
  • Exit Strategy: Structured path toward listing on the Nasdaq Stockholm exchange.
