01 / The Opportunity

TOKE is a utility token engineered to solve slow settlements and high processing costs in merchant payments. Built on the Ethereum L2 Base Network, TOKE bridges the gap between digital asset efficiency and brick-and-mortar retail, enabling instant transactions with processing costs reduced by more than 50% compared to legacy credit card processing systems. TOKE is currently offering early liquidity providers participation in network transaction fee revenues to establish its foundational liquidity pool.

02 / Strategic Moats & Expansion
  • Targeted Market Strategy: Initial rollout focuses on sectors with severe payment friction—specifically North American high-fee industries (including regulated cannabis, representing 100,000+ merchant locations)—before scaling into mainstream retail markets.
  • Consumer-Facing Friction Elimination: A proprietary wallet architecture enables users to create a secure Web3 wallet in seconds using only an email address or mobile number, delivering a familiar Web2 experience (similar to PayPal or Cash App).
  • Broad Merchant Ecosystem: Integrating with point-of-sale (POS) terminal manufacturers and payment processors to deploy TOKE natively across retail checkout terminals worldwide.
03 / Sustainable Tokenomics & LP Utility
  • Multiple Value Capture Streams: The protocol captures value through merchant processing fees, trading volume on secondary markets, and the appreciation of the underlying liquidity pool.
  • Downside Protection & Growth: A dedicated portion of all commercial transaction processing fees is automatically routed back to the liquidity pool, strengthening token stability and driving compounding value for early LPs.
  • Early Allocator Advantage: Liquidity partners receive direct yield from processing fee revenues in exchange for capital commitment.
