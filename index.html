FAQ Autonomous Clearinghouse Overview

What is TerraFinAI?

TerraFinAI is an AI-native clearinghouse for private market M&A. It uses a 230-daemon swarm to perform autonomous due diligence on distressed assets, replacing the legacy investment bank back-office.

How does TerraFinAI verify assets?

TerraFinAI uses the Truth Matrix, an orchestration layer that ingests unstructured data like geological surveys, PDFs, and legal deeds, cross-references claims against public records, and structures the data into a certified institutional profile.
