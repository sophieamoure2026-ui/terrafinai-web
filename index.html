FAQ Clearinghouse Overview

What is TerraFinAI?

TerraFinAI is an AI-native clearinghouse for private market M&A. TerraFinAI performs diligence on ultra-luxury and trophy commercial assets — single-asset mandates where conventional brokerage leaves pricing power on the table.

How does TerraFinAI verify assets?

TerraFinAI is a private clearinghouse for off-market real estate and private M&A. Sellers and brokers submit deal documents; we structure them into clean institutional profiles and match each mandate against a qualified-buyer network of family offices, funds, and institutions.

What is the cost of TerraFinAI?

Fees depend on the engagement and are always agreed in writing before work begins. Buy-side diligence is quoted per mandate, based on asset complexity and data-room size. Sell-side mandates carry a success-based fee set out in the engagement letter. VIP Circle subscriptions are billed annually per tier, and are separate from any transaction-related fees. Submitting intake or requesting an invitation costs nothing. For a fee quote on a specific mandate, email gary@terrafinai.com — we reply within 1 business day.

Are you a broker-dealer?

No. TerraFinAI is a technology platform for off-market real estate intelligence — we are not a registered broker-dealer, financial adviser, or licensed real estate appraiser. We do not execute transactions, hold client funds, or give investment advice. What we deliver is access and structure: qualified-buyer matching, structured deal materials, and diligence support for $100M+ off-market assets. Any transaction is executed by you and your own licensed advisers, on terms you negotiate directly.

What happens after I submit intake?

Within 1 business day you receive a confirmation from our team. Accepted mandates move to profiling: we structure the submitted materials into a clean institutional profile and begin buyer matching. If a mandate isn't a fit, we tell you why, plainly.

How are fees charged?

Fees depend on the engagement type and are always stated in writing before work begins: buy-side diligence is quoted per mandate, based on asset complexity and data-room size; sell-side mandates carry a success-based fee agreed in the engagement letter — never added silently on top of a listing price; VIP Circle subscriptions are annual membership fees per tier, separate from any transaction-related fees. No fees are charged for submitting intake or requesting an invitation.

How are buyer matches assessed?

Every listing carries a buyer fit label — our qualitative read on how closely a mandate matches active buy-side demand across asset class, geography, ticket size, and mandate criteria. Labels reflect our team's current assessment, not a guarantee of outcome. Every introduction is handled directly by our team, not an automated queue.

What is the VIP subscription?

The TerraFinAI Circle is an application-only membership for active buy-side participants, with three tiers — Circle Access ($15K/year), Circle Priority ($35K/year), and Chairman's Circle ($100K/year, capped at 50 members). Members receive deal-flow previews, priority matching, and direct desk access. Membership is currently in waitlist — request an invitation and we will contact you when your tier opens.

Do you work with sell-side brokers?

Yes. Brokers and advisers with qualified $100M+ mandates can submit assets through the same intake protocol — select "Sell-side mandate" as your mandate classification. Accepted mandates receive a structured institutional profile and placement before our buy-side network. For portfolio or multi-asset conversations, email the founder directly: gary@terrafinai.com.
